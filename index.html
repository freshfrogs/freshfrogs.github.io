Fresh Frogs NFT

4,040 Unique Frogs

Fresh Frogs is an Ethereum NFT collection of 4,040 randomly generated frogs, each unique across dozens of frog types and traits. Stake your frogs in The Pond to earn $FLYZ rewards — the longer you stake, the higher your level.

Protecting Real Frogs

A 5% royalty on every sale is split between RainforestUS and Save The Frogs, two nonprofits protecting rainforest habitat and amphibian populations, with the rest reinvested in the project.
